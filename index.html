Responsive treatment-page direction · Desktop view

01 / Challenge

Make a complex decision feel calm.

Treatment pages often ask visitors to process technical information before they feel emotionally ready. The concept reorganises the journey around reassurance, clarity and informed choice.

02 / Response

Lead with confidence, then earn the click.

The page pairs concise, human copy with progressive disclosure: concerns first, treatment logic second and a consultation CTA only when the visitor has enough context.
